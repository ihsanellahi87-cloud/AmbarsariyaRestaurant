# Ambarsariya Restaurant Website

Welcome to the Ambarsariya Restaurant website! This is an authentic Punjabi food restaurant based in Gulberg, Lahore.

## Features

- **Menu Section**: Browse our delicious dishes including Hareesa, Qeema Naan, and BBQ Platters
- **Shopping Cart**: Add items to cart and calculate total price
- **Gallery**: View photos of our restaurant and dishes
- **WhatsApp Ordering**: Direct ordering through WhatsApp integration
- **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on all devices

## How to Order

1. Browse the menu and add items to your cart
2. Click "Checkout" to place your order
3. You'll be directed to WhatsApp where our team will confirm your order

## Custom Domain Setup

To add a custom domain (e.g., `ambarsariyarestaurant.com`):

1. Go to your repository settings
2. Navigate to **Pages** section
3. Add your custom domain in the "Custom domain" field
4. Update your domain's DNS settings to point to GitHub Pages
